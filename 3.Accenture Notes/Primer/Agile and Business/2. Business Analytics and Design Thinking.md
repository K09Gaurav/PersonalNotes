- D. Prescriptive Layer  

---

---

#### Explanation

##### Why A is Correct — Predictive Layer
Predictive analytics focuses on:
- Forecasting future outcomes
- Predicting trends
- Estimating probabilities

- What could happen?
- What is likely to happen next?

Predictive models commonly use:
- Historical data
- Machine learning
- Statistical analysis

---

- What happened
- What is happening

- Simulate human thinking
- Understand complex patterns
- Use AI-based reasoning

##### Why D is Wrong — Prescriptive Layer
Prescriptive analytics answers:
- What should be done?

It recommends actions rather than simply predicting outcomes.

- Operational performance
- Decision-making needs

- A broad and complete view
- Coverage of multiple business aspects

- B. Immerse  
- C. Engage  
- D. Ideate  

---

#### Answer
**A. Observer**

---

#### Explanation

##### Why A is Correct — Observer
Observer activities involve:
- Watching users in real environments
- Analyzing behaviors
- Understanding interactions with surroundings

This helps uncover:
- Hidden problems
- Real user habits
- Unspoken needs

Observation is one of the core empathy research methods.

---

##### Why B is Wrong — Immerse
Immerse means:
- Personally experiencing the user's situation
- Putting yourself in the user's environment

It is different from directly observing user interaction.

---

##### Why C is Wrong — Engage
Engage involves:
- Direct interaction with users
- Interviews and conversations

The question specifically mentions analyzing interactions, which aligns more with observation.

---

##### Why D is Wrong — Ideate
Ideate is:
- The solution brainstorming phase

It is not part of empathy research activities.
