## Question 1

### Question
What distinguishes Generative AI from Discriminative AI?

- A. Generative focuses on labeling, Discriminative on generating
- B. Both are the same
- C. Generative models data distribution, while Discriminative models the boundary between classes
- D. Generative is older, Discriminative is newer
- E. Generative is for images, Discriminative for text

---

### Answer
**C. Generative models data distribution, while Discriminative models the boundary between classes**

---

### Explanation

#### Why C is Correct
The fundamental difference is:

### Generative AI
Generative models learn:
- How data is created
- The underlying probability distribution of data

They can:
- Generate new text
- Create images
- Produce audio
- Simulate new content

Examples:
- GPT
- Gemini
- DALL-E
- Stable Diffusion

### Discriminative AI
Discriminative models learn:
- How to distinguish between categories
- The boundary between classes

They are mainly used for:
- Classification
- Prediction
- Labeling

Examples:
- Spam detection
- Image classification
- Sentiment analysis

---

#### Why A is Wrong
This reverses the actual definitions.

Generative AI generates data, while discriminative models often perform labeling and classification.

---

#### Why B is Wrong
Generative and Discriminative AI are fundamentally different approaches.

---

#### Why D is Wrong
The distinction is based on functionality, not age.

Both approaches have existed for decades.

---

#### Why E is Wrong
Both approaches can work with:
- Images
- Text
- Audio
- Other data types

The difference is not based on media type.

- Predicting continuous values

- Rewards
- Penalties
- Interaction with environments

- Creating new content
- Learning data distributions

Not labeling data.
