# Pre Quiz

## Question 1

### Question

What does Opex stand for in the context of IT expenditures?

- A. Online Expenditure
- B. Optimal Expense
- C. Operating Expenditure
- D. Operational Experience

---

### Answer

**C. Operating Expenditure**

---

### Explanation

#### Why Correct Answer is Correct

Operating Expenditure (Opex) refers to ongoing costs incurred during normal business operations.

In cloud computing, organizations typically pay for:

- Computing resources
- Storage
- Networking
- Managed services

on a subscription or pay-as-you-go basis.

Cloud adoption often converts large upfront investments into operational expenses.

---

#### Why Option A is Wrong

Online Expenditure is not a recognized financial term.

---

#### Why Option B is Wrong

Optimal Expense is not a standard accounting term.

---

#### Why Option C is Correct

Opex officially stands for Operating Expenditure.

---

#### Why Option D is Wrong

Operational Experience is unrelated to financial accounting.

---

### Exam Tip

Cloud Computing → Mostly Opex

Traditional Data Centers → Mostly Capex

- A. Increased scalability
- B. Cost savings
- C. Enhanced security and control
- D. Greater flexibility in resource allocation

---

### Answer

**C. Enhanced security and control**

---

- Greater control over infrastructure
- Better security management
- Easier compliance with regulations
- Customized security policies

---

#### Why Option A is Wrong

Private clouds can scale, but public clouds generally offer greater scalability.

---

#### Why Option B is Wrong

Private clouds are usually more expensive because the organization owns or exclusively uses the infrastructure.

---

#### Why Option C is Correct

Security and control are the primary advantages of private cloud environments.

---

#### Why Option D is Wrong

Flexibility exists, but security and control are the defining benefits.

---

### Exam Tip

Private Cloud = Security + Control
