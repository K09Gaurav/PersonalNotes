# Pre Quiz

## Question 1

### Question
What are the two main components of a GAN?

- A. Encoder and Decoder
- B. Input and Output
- C. None of the given options
- D. Forward and Backward
- E. Generator and Discriminator

---

### Answer
**Generator and Discriminator**

---

### Explanation

#### Why Correct Answer is Correct
GAN stands for **Generative Adversarial Network**. It consists of two neural networks that compete against each other:

- **Generator**: Creates synthetic data that resembles real data.
- **Discriminator**: Evaluates whether the data is real or generated.

The generator attempts to fool the discriminator, while the discriminator attempts to correctly identify fake data. This adversarial process improves both networks over time and allows the generator to produce highly realistic outputs.

Important exam point:
- GAN = Generator + Discriminator

---

#### Why Option A is Wrong
Encoder and Decoder are typically associated with **Autoencoders** and **Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)**, not GANs.

---

#### Why Option B is Wrong
Input and Output are general terms used in all machine learning models and are not the defining components of GANs.

---

#### Why Option C is Wrong
A correct option is present, namely Generator and Discriminator.

---

#### Why Option D is Wrong
Forward and Backward refer to stages of neural network training, not the components of a GAN.

---

#### Why Option E is Correct
Generator and Discriminator are the two fundamental networks that make up a GAN.
