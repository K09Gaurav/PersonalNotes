## Question 1

### Question
What is the primary goal of generative models in AI?

- A. To reduce computational cost
- B. To generate new data samples
- C. To classify data
- D. To optimize algorithms
- E. To analyze data distributions

---

### Answer
**To generate new data samples**

---

### Explanation

#### Why Correct Answer is Correct
Generative models are designed to learn the underlying data distribution and generate new samples that resemble the training data.

Examples:
- Generate images
- Produce text
- Create audio
- Synthesize videos

The focus is creation rather than prediction.

Important exam points:
- Generative → learns data distribution
- Output → new synthetic data

---

#### Why Option A is Wrong
Reducing computational cost is not the primary objective.

---

#### Why Option B is Correct
Generating realistic new data is the defining goal of generative models.

---

#### Why Option C is Wrong
Classification is mainly associated with discriminative models.

---

#### Why Option D is Wrong
Optimization supports training but is not the purpose.

---

#### Why Option E is Wrong
Analyzing distributions may occur internally but generation is the primary goal.

## Question 2

### Question
In the context of models, what does P(x | y) typically represent?

- A. The likelihood of y
- B. The probability of y given x
- C. The probability of x given y
- D. The generative capacity of x
- E. The distribution of y

---

### Answer
**The probability of x given y**

---

### Explanation

#### Why Correct Answer is Correct
The notation:

**P(x | y)**

means:

> Probability of x occurring given that y is known.

This is called conditional probability.

Examples:
- Probability of an image given a label
- Probability of a sentence given a topic

Important exam points:
- P(A | B) → Probability of A given B
- Read from left to right.

---

#### Why Option A is Wrong
Likelihood is a related concept but not the direct interpretation.

---

#### Why Option B is Wrong
This reverses the conditional direction.

---

#### Why Option C is Correct
This is the correct definition of conditional probability notation.

---

#### Why Option D is Wrong
Generative capacity is not represented this way.

---

#### Why Option E is Wrong
Distribution of y would be written differently.

- One of the most famous GAN training problems
- Reduced output diversity
