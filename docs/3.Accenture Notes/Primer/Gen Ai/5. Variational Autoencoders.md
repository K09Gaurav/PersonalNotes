## Question 1

### Question
What is the primary role of autoencoders in generative modeling?

- A. Data compression and reconstruction
- B. Regression
- C. Data classification
- D. Image recognition
- E. Clustering

---

### Answer
**Data compression and reconstruction**

---

### Explanation

#### Why Correct Answer is Correct
Autoencoders are neural networks designed to learn compact representations of data and reconstruct the original input from those representations.

They consist of:
- Encoder → compresses input into latent representation
- Decoder → reconstructs original data

In generative modeling, this learned latent space enables generation of similar samples.

Important exam points:
- Encoder → Compression
- Decoder → Reconstruction
- Latent space → Compact representation

---

#### Why Option A is Correct
Compression and reconstruction define the core purpose of autoencoders.

---

#### Why Option B is Wrong
Regression predicts continuous values.

---

#### Why Option C is Wrong
Classification is not the primary objective.

---

#### Why Option D is Wrong
Image recognition is a separate task.

---

#### Why Option E is Wrong
Clustering groups similar observations.

- A. It reduces the model's complexity
- B. It increases the model's accuracy
- C. It allows backpropagation through stochastic nodes
- D. It reduces the need for labeled data
- E. It speeds up the training process

---

#### Why Correct Answer is Correct
VAEs sample latent variables, but sampling operations are not directly differentiable.

The reparameterization trick rewrites sampling into a differentiable operation, allowing gradients to flow during training.

Conceptually:
- Separate randomness
- Keep computation differentiable

Important exam points:
- Sampling blocks gradients.
- Reparameterization enables backpropagation.

---
