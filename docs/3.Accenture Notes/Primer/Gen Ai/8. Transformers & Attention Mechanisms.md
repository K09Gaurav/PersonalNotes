# Pre Quiz


## Question 1

### Question
The Transformer architecture introduced the concept of self-attention to handle which primary challenge in sequence modeling?

- A. Handling larger input sizes
- B. Reducing model size
- C. Capturing dependencies regardless of their distance in the input
- D. Improving model robustness
- E. Speeding up training

---

### Answer
**Capturing dependencies regardless of their distance in the input**

---

### Explanation

#### Why Correct Answer is Correct
Traditional RNNs and LSTMs often struggle to model relationships between words that are far apart in a sequence.

Self-attention allows every token to directly interact with every other token.

Example:
- "The book that I borrowed yesterday was interesting."
- The model can directly relate "book" and "interesting" even if many words separate them.

Important exam points:
- Self-attention captures long-range dependencies.
- Distance between words becomes less problematic.

---

#### Why Option A is Wrong
Handling larger inputs is a benefit but not the primary motivation.

---

#### Why Option B is Wrong
Transformers are generally large models.

---

#### Why Option C is Correct
This is the central problem self-attention was designed to solve.

---

#### Why Option D is Wrong
Robustness is not the primary objective.

---

#### Why Option E is Wrong
Transformers also improve parallelization, but dependency modeling is the key reason.

---
